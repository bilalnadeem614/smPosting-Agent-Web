One place to queue and publish your own posts.

smPosting is a personal scheduling tool that takes a video or image with a caption and a time, and publishes it to your own connected social accounts — automatically, without you opening each app.

01

Send

You send the content, caption, and either a time or "now" through a private control channel.

02

Queue

The post is checked against each platform's requirements and held until its scheduled time.

03

Publish

At the right time, it's published directly to your connected accounts, and you're notified.

About this tool

smPosting is built and operated by a single person for their own accounts. It does not manage content for other users, and it does not read, collect, or act on data belonging to anyone other than its owner.

It currently supports scheduled and immediate publishing to TikTok, alongside other connected platforms, from one queue.
